This blog is a static site: it has no login, no database, and no form of its own, and it **does not use cookies**. The only third-party services are the ones listed below.

## Traffic statistics: Cloudflare Web Analytics

The blog uses [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to count visits and measure page performance. It **does not use cookies**, does not store data in your browser, and does not identify you individually. See the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

The blog is also served through the Cloudflare network (proxy, cache, and security), which processes technical connection data, such as the IP address, to deliver the pages and block attacks.

## Comments: giscus and GitHub Discussions

New comments use [giscus](https://giscus.app), which stores each comment in this blog's [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/aeciopires/blog-aeciopires/discussions). To comment, you sign in with your GitHub account.

- The comment, your username, and your profile picture are **public on GitHub**.
- You can edit or delete your comment on GitHub itself.
- The handling of this data follows the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/pt/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The comments area is only loaded when you scroll down to it.

## Archived comments from the old blog

Comments made on the old blog (WordPress) appear as read-only text. Only the name provided by the comment author, the date, and the text were kept. **The author's email, IP, browser, and website were not migrated.** If you wrote one of these comments and want it removed, use the [contact]({{< relref "/contato" >}}) form.

## Contact form: Jotform

The [Contact]({{< relref "/contato" >}}) page uses a [Jotform](https://www.jotform.com) form. The data you enter is sent to Jotform and delivered to me. See the [Jotform privacy policy](https://www.jotform.com/privacy/).

## Embedded videos

Some posts embed YouTube videos through the `youtube-nocookie.com` domain. The video is only loaded when you click it.

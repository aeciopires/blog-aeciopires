[English Version]

Hot news for those who have installed [zabbix](https://www.zabbix.com/) on [kubernetes](https://kubernetes.io/).

[Christian Anton](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christiananton1/) and I have just released versions 7.0.1 and 7.0.0 of the Zabbix [helm](https://helm.sh/) Chart! 🚀

They offer many improvements and one important change. Read the changes here: [https://artifacthub.io/packages/helm/zabbix-community/zabbix#version-7-0-0](https://artifacthub.io/packages/helm/zabbix-community/zabbix#version-7-0-0) 🎉

Since 2022, this helm chart has almost 300,000 downloads ([https://tooomm.github.io/github-release-stats/?username=zabbix-community&repository=helm-zabbix](https://tooomm.github.io/github-release-stats/?username=zabbix-community&repository=helm-zabbix)).

We thank [Zabbix](https://www.linkedin.com/company/zabbix/) for maintaining and making this free and open source software available, and the people who submit pull requests, report bugs, and suggest improvements to the helm chart.

Special thanks to [Christian Anton](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christiananton1/), who has created many technical solutions to reported issues and helps me with code management.

Be sure to check out the latest updates and share your feedback:

Notícia quentinha para quem instala [zabbix](https://www.zabbix.com/) no [kubernetes](https://kubernetes.io/).  
[Christian Anton](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christiananton1/) e eu lançamos há pouco as versões 7.0.1 e 7.0.0 do [helm](https://helm.sh/) Chart Zabbix! 🚀  
Elas oferecem muitas melhorias e uma mudança incompatível (_breaking change_) importante. Leia com calma as mudanças detalhadas aqui: [https://lnkd.in/dpV5zbmC](https://lnkd.in/dpV5zbmC) 🎉

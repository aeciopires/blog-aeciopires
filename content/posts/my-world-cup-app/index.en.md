Brazil didn't win its sixth title this time, but I'm so excited watching the games of this World Cup that, between thrilling matches (provided by Cape Verde, Egypt, Congo, Curaçao, Croatia, Portugal, Iran, Argentina :-P ) and controversial refereeing decisions, I created a web app to show the match results. This was my nerdy version of the printed match chart I had as a kid.

I hope you like it. More details at: [https://github.com/aeciopires/my-world-cup-app](https://github.com/aeciopires/my-world-cup-app)

The World Cup is almost over, but the knowledge I gained while developing this app will stay with me for a long time.

[![logo%20COMPLETO%2020cm%20RGB](/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/logo-COMPLETO-20cm-RGB-1024x485.jpg)](http://portasabertas.org.br) In some places around the world, the persecution of Christians is much stronger. The [Portas Abertas](http://portasabertas.org.br) (Open Doors) mission exists to help Christians in their struggles to keep going without losing their faith and still be able to share their life testimony with other people. Visit the [Mission's website](http://portasabertas.org.br) (in Portuguese), learn about these people's life testimonies, and contribute. There are many ways to help.

Hello! André Déo, Adail Spinola, and I are the authors of the book "[De A a Zabbix](http://www.novatec.com.br/livros/zabbix/)". If you haven't read the book yet or want to learn more about Zabbix ([http://zabbix.com](http://zabbix.com)), you can attend an in-person course with one of us. **The courses are in person**, held on different dates and in different cities, according to each author's availability (see the syllabus, price, and other information at the links below).

**Course with Adail:** [http://spinola.net.br/blog/curso-de-a-a-zabbix/](http://spinola.net.br/blog/curso-de-a-a-zabbix/) **Course with me:**  **Course with André:** [http://andredeo.blogspot.com.br/2016/05/curso-de-a-zabbix.html](http://andredeo.blogspot.com.br/2016/05/curso-de-a-zabbix.html) **Course at CT-Novatec with one of the three authors:** [http://ctnovatec.com.br/cursos/trilha-redes-de-computadores/curso-monitoracao-de-redes-com-zabbix/](http://ctnovatec.com.br/cursos/trilha-redes-de-computadores/curso-monitoracao-de-redes-com-zabbix/)

The courses are taught with the same quality standard that led many readers to trust what is written in the book, blogs, websites, and slides, as well as the everyday support each of us provides in the Zabbix-BR community ([http://zabbixbrasil.org](http://zabbixbrasil.org)).

The idea is to extend the experience written in the book, with more examples, exercises, greater detail, and the exchange of experiences. Hugs, and God bless.

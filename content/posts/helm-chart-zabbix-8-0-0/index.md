[English Version]

Hot news for those who have installed [zabbix](https://www.zabbix.com/) on [kubernetes](https://kubernetes.io/).

I have just released version 8.0.0 of the Zabbix [helm](https://helm.sh/) Chart! 🚀

This version offers many improvements. Read the changes here: [https://artifacthub.io/packages/helm/zabbix-community/zabbix#version-8-0-0](https://artifacthub.io/packages/helm/zabbix-community/zabbix#version-8-0-0) 🎉

Since 2022, this helm chart has had almost 2,000,000 downloads ([https://tooomm.github.io/github-release-stats/?username=zabbix-community&repository=helm-zabbix](https://tooomm.github.io/github-release-stats/?username=zabbix-community&repository=helm-zabbix)).

We thank [Zabbix](https://www.linkedin.com/company/zabbix/) for maintaining and making this free and open source software available, and the people who submit pull requests, report bugs, and suggest improvements to the helm chart.

Special thanks to [Christian Anton](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christiananton1/) with code management.

Be sure to check out the latest updates and share your feedback:  
👉 [https://github.com/zabbix-community/helm-zabbix/releases](https://github.com/zabbix-community/helm-zabbix/releases)  
👉 [https://artifacthub.io/packages/helm/zabbix-community/zabbix](https://artifacthub.io/packages/helm/zabbix-community/zabbix)

[![A_a_Zabbix](/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/A_a_Zabbix.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/A_a_Zabbix.png) Since 2011, [Adail Spínola](http://spinola.net.br/blog/), [André Déo](http://andredeo.blogspot.com.br/), and I have been writing a book about Zabbix. Thank God, it is now expected to be published next month. :-)

Learn more about the book here (in Portuguese): [http://zabbixone.com/?p=68](http://zabbixone.com/?p=68)

Novatec's website is promoting the book at the bottom of its home page: [http://www.novatec.com.br/](http://www.novatec.com.br/)

The book is supported by [Alexei Vladishev](https://twitter.com/avladishev), the creator of Zabbix, and Luciano Alves, owner of [UNIREDE](http://www.unirede.net/), the company that represents Zabbix in Latin America and administers the Zabbix certification in Brazil.

It is not yet possible to buy the book through presale, and I don't know the estimated price yet, but you will find out very soon. Stay tuned and follow the news on the book's website: [http://zabbixone.com](http://zabbixone.com).

If you are a student or teacher in the field of computer networks, the book can be used in network management and monitoring courses. The book's content is also in line with the topics covered by the Zabbix certification.

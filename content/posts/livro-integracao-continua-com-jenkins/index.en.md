This morning, [Novatec](https://novatec.com.br/livros/jenkins) published a new book (in Portuguese), written by me and Janaína Militão, about using Jenkins integrated with tools that support the DevOps culture, such as Gogs, Docker, Sonarqube, Terraform and Nexus.

The goal of the book is to teach how to integrate these tools to build an automated continuous integration, delivery and deployment process for one or more applications.

The book also has a dedicated website, available at: [https://jenkins.aeciopires.com](https://jenkins.aeciopires.com)

I wish you a good read and I hope the book helps you in your day-to-day work.
